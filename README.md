# ZioNotes

Records your meetings and writes the notes: transcript, summary, action items.
Everything stays on your computer.

## Download

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): [ZioNotes-mac.dmg](https://github.com/operations-netizen/zionotes-releases/releases/latest/download/ZioNotes-mac.dmg)
- **Windows**: [ZioNotes-windows-setup.exe](https://github.com/operations-netizen/zionotes-releases/releases/latest/download/ZioNotes-windows-setup.exe)

Every release, with its notes: [Releases](https://github.com/operations-netizen/zionotes-releases/releases)

## Installing

**Mac.** Open the .dmg and drag ZioNotes into Applications. The first time you
open it, macOS may say it cannot check the app for malicious software. Open
*System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down, and click
**Open Anyway** next to ZioNotes.

**Windows.** Run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

After that, ZioNotes updates itself. When a new version is ready, the sidebar
shows **Restart to update**.
