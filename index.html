ZioNotes

Your meetings, written up for you.

ZioNotes records the call, writes the transcript, summary and action items, and keeps all of it on your computer. No bot joins your meeting.

Download ZioNotes

Free for everyone at the company. Updates install themselves.

Mac

  1. Open ZioNotes-mac.dmg and drag ZioNotes into Applications.
  2. Open ZioNotes. The first time, macOS says it can’t check the app for malicious software. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to ZioNotes.
  4. Allow the Microphone and Screen Recording when asked.

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later). Download for Mac

Windows

  1. Run ZioNotes-windows-setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. No administrator rights are needed.

Windows 10 and 11. Download for Windows

After the first install you never download it again: when a new version is ready, ZioNotes shows Restart to update in its sidebar.
